# MK-02 to MK-05 — Landing page

**Goal:** a landing page that sells PaperCut in five seconds on a phone. It should show the swipe, say why the citations can be trusted, and get people into `/app` without signing up.

## Sections

1. **Hero (MK-02).** A teal-700 band with cream type: "Swipe right on research.", one supporting line, the primary CTA "Try it — no signup" → `/app`, and a secondary "How it works ↓". Beside it, an animated stack of six real fixture papers that swipe themselves (save, save, skip) with SAVED/SKIP stamps.
2. **How it works (MK-03).** Three numbered steps: brief → swipe → export.
3. **Features (MK-03).** A 2×2 / 4-up grid: brief-aware, real citations, group mode (labelled **Coming soon** until GR-* ships), accessible.
4. **Trust (MK-04).** "Every citation is real." This explains retrieval-only, in two lists: what comes from the source (bibliographic data, BibTeX, credibility) and what Claude writes (overview, why it matters, stance).
5. **Closing CTA and footer.**

## MK-05

- **OG/Twitter image:** `(marketing)/opengraph-image.tsx`, 1200×630, static, with an alt text.
- **Icons:** `app/icon.svg` and `app/apple-icon.tsx` (the scissors mark), replacing the scaffold `favicon.ico`.
- **Metadata:** og:type, site name, en_GB locale, `summary_large_image`.
- **Brand colours** for generated images live in `components/marketing/brand-colors.ts`, because Satori can't read CSS variables.

## Accessibility

- The demo stack is `aria-hidden`; the hero copy carries the message.
- It is static under `prefers-reduced-motion`, and otherwise has a **Pause demo** toggle (WCAG 2.2.2).
- Controls on teal use a cream focus ring, and every target is at least 44px.
- One `h1`, then `h2` per section and `h3` per item.

## Out of scope

MK-01 (design-system extension), marketing illustrations, a custom domain.
